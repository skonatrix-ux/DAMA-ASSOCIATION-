    The Dama Approach

    1. Count captures first. The longest jump is compulsory, for both sides. Scan for forced sequences before every move.
    2. Force their hand. Since captures are mandatory, set traps where whatever they must take costs them more.
    3. Sacrifice one to take two. Bait with a piece to open a bigger capture for yourself.
    4. Own the edges. Pieces on side files can't be jumped horizontally, they make safe anchors.
    5. Guard your back rank. Keep a piece home as long as you can to block enemy kings.
    6. Push for a king. One flying king usually decides the endgame.
    7. Don't clump the middle. A packed center gets eaten in forced chains.
